    An outlet you read behind its paywall in a month counts its cheapest digital subscription for that month, once, however many of its articles you read: the regular price, not the introductory offer. Each price links to where it comes from; an outlet with none found isn't counted. The count is kept on this computer and never sent anywhere.

    Where Read Free takes the walls down. A site turned off is left as it sends its pages.

      Notifications

      Read Free says so whenever a wall comes off. Turned off, it speaks up only when it has something new to say after your first few articles: the first time you read an outlet, why it's boycotted, and now and then a link to donate. The ✓ on its toolbar icon always shows when a wall came off.

      For articles a site never sends in full. archive.today answers under several names. If the one in use doesn't answer, one other is tried; if it keeps failing (some networks and DNS services block it), pick another.

      Saved articles

      Articles whose text Read Free puts in are kept on this computer for 30 days (up to 50 MB, oldest dropped first), so reloads and revisits are instant. Nothing read in an incognito or private window is saved.
