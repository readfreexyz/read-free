Read Free is on

Open a paywalled article from one of the outlets below and Read Free takes the wall off. The first time you read each outlet, it tells you why it's boycotted, with the source.

Three things to know

  1. Reload any news tabs that were already open. Read Free works on pages opened from now on.
  2. Leave Developer mode on in chrome://extensions. With it off, Chrome turns Read Free off.
  3. Nothing to update by hand. Firefox installs new versions of Read Free by itself.
  4. Can't see the watermelon next to the address bar? Click the puzzle piece there and pin Read Free. Its button shows what it did on each page, and its settings.
